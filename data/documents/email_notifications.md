# Email Notifications

MyDailyBlog sends transactional and account-related emails asynchronously in the background.

## Common Emails

Users may receive emails for:

- Registration welcome.
- Password reset.
- Profile updates.
- Post creation, update, or deletion confirmations.
- Donation appreciation (after a successful donation).
- Occasional re-engagement reminders after 10 days of inactivity.

## Delayed or Missing Emails

Emails are processed in the background and may take a few minutes to arrive. If an expected email does not arrive:

- Check spam or junk folders.
- Confirm the correct email address is associated with the account.
- Wait a few minutes.
- Contact support if the issue continues.

## Password Reset Emails

Password reset emails contain a tokenized link that should be treated as confidential and not shared with anyone.
