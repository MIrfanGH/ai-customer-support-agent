# Troubleshooting

## Cannot Log In

1. Check that Caps Lock is off.
2. Confirm the username and password are correct.
3. Use the password reset flow from the login page.
4. Contact support if you still cannot access the account.

## Password Reset Email Missing

- Check spam or junk folders.
- Confirm the account email address is correct.
- Wait briefly and request another reset link.
- Contact support if the problem persists.

## Cannot Create a Post

Only users with the Author role can create posts. If you see a permission error when trying to create a post, your account is likely set to Reader. Contact support to request Author access.

## Profile Image Not Updating

Profile images are optimized in the background after upload. If the new image does not appear immediately:

- Wait a short time and refresh the page.
- Try uploading a smaller image.
- Contact support if the issue continues.

## AI Summary Not Appearing

- Refresh the page and wait for background generation to finish.
- Confirm you haven't exceeded the summary rate limit (5 requests per 60 seconds).
- Confirm the post has content.
- Contact support if the summary remains unavailable.

## Donation Not Confirmed

- Do not make another donation immediately.
- Check the donation status if visible.
- Check your email for a confirmation.
- Contact support with your payment email and approximate payment time.
